## 1. The SP+ blocker was wrong — correction of record

The v0.4 claim that ESPN's SP+ page was "Insider-paywalled; only a prose
article with scattered individual team ranks" was **incorrect**. The
official ESPN article — "2026 college football SP+ rankings for all 138
FBS teams" (story id 48306284, published 2026-03-27T10:33Z per the page's
own `datePublished` metadata) — contains the complete 138-team table, and
that table was present in the raw capture archived on 2026-07-19 all
along: two HTML tables (139-row team table with columns Team / SP+ /
Off. SP+ / Def. SP+ / ST SP+, plus an 11-row conference table). The error
was in the v0.4 build's text extraction, which flattened the article's
HTML tables into prose before assessing it — an extraction bug, not a
property of the source. (The capture's archived filename contains the
erroneous `_PAYWALLED_` label; the file itself is byte-identical to the
original capture and is included here under `raw_sources/` unmodified for
provenance.)

Every workbook statement claiming SP+ was paywalled/unavailable/incomplete
has been removed or corrected: `START HERE!A3`, `PRESEASON!A1`, and
CHANGELOG rows 21 (the blocker entry), 25 (the user-decision entry's SP+
portion), and 29 (the v0.4.1 stale-text entry's SP+ mention). The
corrected entries explicitly record what the original text claimed and why
it was wrong.
