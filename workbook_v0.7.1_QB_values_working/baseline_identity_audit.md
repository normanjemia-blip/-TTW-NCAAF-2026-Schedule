## Method

The binding (earliest, largest-weight) snapshot is **SP+, 2026-03-27**. A team was initialized at 0/0 only if its current Active QB was reasonably the projected starter priced into that snapshot AND the two current (2026-07-19) snapshots — i.e., a returning starter or a winter-portal transfer (portal closed ~Jan 2026, before 3/27), with no unresolved injury/eligibility/availability issue and no material change after the snapshots. The 2026 cycle had no spring transfer window, so essentially all transfers predate 3/27 and are reflected in all three sources. Two borderline eligibility/injury cases were verified by targeted search:

- **Ole Miss (Chambliss):** eligibility granted by the court in **February 2026** (before the 3/27 SP+ snapshot) and the NCAA appeal was **denied** — final and priced in -> INITIALIZED.
- **North Carolina (Edwards Jr.):** **still recovering** from a torn PCL (expected healthy by camp) and only holds the "inside track" — unresolved availability + unsettled starter -> EXCEPTION.
