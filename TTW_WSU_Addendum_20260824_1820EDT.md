**15:18 PDT = 18:18 EDT** — two minutes before this addendum's check, and **2h 24m after** the memo's
15:54 EDT cutoff (12:54 PDT). The memo fetched the beat outlet's own article index at 15:54 EDT and
recorded that nothing dated Aug 23 or 24 appeared. That observation was accurate.

> **Stated honestly:** if that stamp were instead rendered in Eastern time, publication would have been
> 15:18 EDT — 36 minutes *before* the memo's cutoff, and the index simply had not surfaced it yet.
> I cannot resolve the site's display timezone from the page itself, so I am not asserting which it is.
> **Either reading leaves the memo's recorded observation factually correct**, and either way the
> ruling is now superseded. The memo also named this exact outcome as the most likely next step and
> set the correct recheck trigger.
