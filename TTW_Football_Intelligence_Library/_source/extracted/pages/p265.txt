2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
It’s football season, yet the unveiling of a new-look Pac-12 Conference brings back memories of the late, great Bill Walton. 
The basketball legend and colorful broadcaster constantly touted the “conference of champions” due to the league’s 
impressive history of NCAA titles. Unfortunately, the schools responsible for almost all of those titles — Stanford, UCLA, 
USC, Cal, Oregon, Washington, Arizona and Arizona State — are long gone and spread to different conferences all over 
the map. Oregon State and Washington State, the league’s two leftovers, have been joined by a handful of newcomers 
from the Mountain West (Boise State, San Diego State, Colorado State, Fresno State and Utah State) and a straggler from 
the Sun Belt (Texas State). The Pac-12 consists of only eight teams and all include the name “State.” The conference will 
not get an automatic qualifier into the College Football Playoff.
Boise State, which won the last three Mountain West championships, is listed by DraftKings as the +170 favorite to win the 
Pac-12. The Broncos could boast the conference’s top quarterback, Maddux Madsen, and top running back, Dylan Riley. 
San Diego State, the +370 second choice, went 9-4 last season and is led by workhorse running back Lucky Sutton.
The league does present a few intriguing teams beyond the Broncos and Aztecs. In his first season at Fresno State, coach 
Matt Entz won nine games. Watch out for Entz and the Bulldogs, and keep an eye on Colorado State with new coach Jim 
Mora Jr., who worked an incredible turnaround at UConn. Texas State arrives with quarterback Brad Jackson triggering 
an explosive passing attack.
The Pac-12 can make a statement, for better or worse, when Boise State opens its season at Oregon on Sept. 5. On the 
same day, Fresno State visits USC and Washington State plays at Washington. Other nonconference matchups to circle are 
San Diego State at UCLA (Sept. 12) and BYU at Colorado State (Sept. 19). In the biggest league games, Fresno hosts Boise 
on Oct. 10 and Boise hosts San Diego State on Nov. 21.
