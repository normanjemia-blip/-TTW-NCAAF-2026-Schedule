Is this the best rush offense in the country?
It sounds hyperbolic, but it might not be an unfair question. First 
and foremost, Missouri has arguably the best running back tandem 
in the country. Ahmad Hardy and Jamal Roberts combined to rush 
for 2,402 yards and 22 touchdowns last season. Hardy (1,649 yards) 
is one of the best backs in the country. He forced 97 missed tackles 
and averaged 4.6 yards per carry after contact last year. Despite 
suffering a gunshot wound in May, he will be ready to roll for fall 
camp. The duo will line up behind a strong offensive line in 2026. 
Left tackle Cayden Green is back, and the Tigers have a total of 118 
career starts in the room. Like last season, this will be the backbone 
of this offense.
Could Austin Simmons be the steal of the transfer portal?
Simmons was named the starting quarterback for Ole Miss last 
season and started the first two games of the year before being 
injured. By the time he was available again, Trinidad Chambliss had 
taken the starting gig. Simmons now gets a new life at Missouri. 
It’s rare that a starting-caliber quarterback is available, especially 
one that didn’t necessarily lose the job due to performance. Having 
said that, Simmons does need to take better care of the football. In 
84 dropbacks last season, he threw five interceptions and posted a 
5.7% turnover-worthy play rate. He’s going to have a strong running 
game to lean on. If he can cut down on putting the ball in danger, he 
might be one of the best pickups of any team in the transfer portal.
Can Eliah Drinkwitz take the next step as a head coach?
It took three seasons, but Drinkwitz has finally transformed Mizzou 
into a winning football program. Over the last three seasons, the 
Tigers are 29-10 SU overall and 15-9 SU in the SEC; 25-14 ATS in 
totality over that span. But what it doesn’t have is an appearance 
in the SEC Championship or the College Football Playoff. It’s a 
difficult season to achieve that goal. Texas and Georgia are primed 
to get to Atlanta, and there are strong contenders on the fringes 
like Alabama, Ole Miss and Texas A&M. Not many of them have the 
potential on offense that a team like Missouri has, though. Perhaps 
this is the season Drinkwitz gets through the ceiling.
