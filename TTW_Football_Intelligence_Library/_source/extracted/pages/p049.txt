2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
The American Conference seems pretty wide-open for the 2026 season and it’s fair to wonder if any team will have the 
record or resume to represent the Group of Six in the College Football Playoff. There are four new head coaches and five 
second-year head coaches in the conference, so nine of the league’s 14 teams are tough to project. Tenured coaches might 
have an inside track this season, but anything could happen.
In terms of preseason conference winner odds, only the MAC has a higher-priced favorite than the American and seven 
of the 14 teams are priced at +800 or lower. Navy is the tepid favorite here with one of the more tenured coaches in the 
conference, as Brian Newberry begins his fourth season at the helm. UTSA’s Jeff Traylor and Army’s Jeff Monken are 
the only two sideline bosses with more years under their belts than Newberry. It probably isn’t a coincidence that UTSA 
(+480) and Army (6-1) are the second and third favorites, respectively, behind Navy (+310).
Life is tough in the American Conference for college football these days. As mentioned, nine of the 14 teams have head 
coaches hired within the last two seasons because this conference is a springboard to Power Four jobs. Furthermore, 
any players that stand out are going to be on the minds of SEC, ACC, Big Ten, and Big 12 teams that are looking to fill 
some voids and plug some holes. In some respects, the service academies have an advantage in that they aren’t watching 
players go out the door for NIL paydays, but they also can’t leverage the transfer portal to their advantage.
Outside of Army and Navy, just about every program in the conference has fewer than 10 returning starters. But, that 
doesn’t mean they are immune to big personnel losses, especially in Navy’s case with QB Blake Horvath.
In a high-variance conference, there should be money to be made, whether you can isolate overachievers or 
underachievers with props and futures before the season or on a weekly basis.
