Pete Golding has no ill-will for

Oxford do. Winning silences all 
of those voices, and Kiffin did 
his part in the offseason to win 
early and often in Baton Rouge.
The Tigers brought in the best 
transfer class in the country, 
and brought key staff members 
in from Ole Miss. Kiffin is an 
elite coach, but success on the
