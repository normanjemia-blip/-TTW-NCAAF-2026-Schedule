2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
Texas Tech stole the headlines in the Big 12 last season, winning the league and grabbing an automatic College Football 
Playoff berth. The Red Raiders did flame out in the postseason, losing 23-0 to Oregon in the quarterfinals. However, 
Joey McGuire’s program officially made its presence felt on the national stage. We also saw a tremendous season out of 
conference runner-up BYU, we witnessed Utah and Houston win double-digit games, and the depth of the league was 
impressive with nine teams finishing with winning records. 
Heading into the 2026 season, the Red Raiders are once again expected to finish on top. However, Texas Tech will have 
to do it without Brendan Sorsby, who was supposed to take the passing game to another level before a gambling scandal 
derailed everything. The Red Raiders will now need to fight off some much-improved teams without an elite signal-caller. 
Texas Tech is an odds-on favorite to win the Big 12 Conference Championship, but BYU and Utah aren’t too far back on 
the oddsboard. Kansas State, Houston, and Arizona are also shorter than 20-1 at DraftKings Sportsbook, and it’d be foolish 
to rule out 2024 conference champion Arizona State. 
This simply should be a fascinating year in what has become one of the most interesting leagues in college football.
