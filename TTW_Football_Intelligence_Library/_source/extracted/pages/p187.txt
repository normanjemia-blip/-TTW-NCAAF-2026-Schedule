2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
Recently, Conference USA has been the proving ground for teams moving up from the FCS ranks to the FBS world. This 
season, that is not the case, as the new teams are in the Mountain West and MAC. However, with Louisiana Tech off to the 
Sun Belt and UTEP to the Mountain West, Conference USA goes from 12 teams down to 10.
This is absolutely the weakest conference in college football. There were 136 FBS teams last season. Jeff Sagarin’s end-
of-season rankings had four teams in the conference 158th or lower. Four other teams were ranked 120th or lower. 
Ironically, the top two teams as ranked by Sagarin - Western Kentucky and Louisiana Tech - weren’t even involved in the 
conference championship game. Kennesaw State won 19-15 over Jacksonville State in that one.
It was a truly impressive season for Kennesaw State. The Owls fired their longtime head coach during their first season 
as a FBS member in 2024 and hired Jerry Mack, who was a running backs coach in the NFL the season prior and had only 
been a head coach at NC Central in the mid-2010s. With that win, the Owls avenged their only conference regular-season 
loss and then got pumped by Western Michigan in the Myrtle Beach Bowl.
The Gamecocks may be able to avenge that title game loss, as they look to be a contender once again behind second-year 
head coach Charles Kelly, as the team lost head coach Rich Rodriguez, had very few returning starters, and still went 7-1 
in league action before the loss in the title game. 
Arguably the biggest storyline of the season is whether or not Liberty will bounce back. After a 21-5 start, including a 
College Football Playoff appearance, it all fell apart for Jamey Chadwell, as the Flames went just 4-8 last season. Liberty 
(+260) is the conference favorite over Western Kentucky (+280), who has the longest-tenured coach in the conference 
as Tyson Helton starts his eighth season. With Jacksonville State (+450) and Delaware (7-1) as the others lined in single 
digits, this looks to be a wide-open Group of Six conference again.
