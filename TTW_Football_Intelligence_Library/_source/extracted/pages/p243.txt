2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
They say success is the greatest revenge, but in this case of jilted lovers, the Mountain West might have some trouble 
making the defectors to the new Pac-12 jealous. Gone are Boise State, San Diego State, Fresno State, Colorado State and 
Utah State. To replace them, the conference brought in UTEP, Northern Illinois and North Dakota State. The Bison have 
a shot to be the hot new fling, but the Miners and Huskies are projected to be cellar-dwellars in their first seasons in the 
conference.
The aforementioned North Dakota State program — which dominated the FCS and the Missouri Valley Football 
Conference — is one of the short shots to win the Mountain West. UNLV and New Mexico are either tied as favorites or 
have slight variance in their prices to win it all, depending on the shop. All three programs have rightful claims to their 
status as true contenders, but the conference does go slightly deeper than that.
Hawaii returns quarterback Micah Alejado, who was named preseason Offensive Player of the Year. Timmy Chang has 
improved his win total each season on the island and has both an effective offense and a favorable schedule in conference 
play. There is no doubt the Rainbow Warriors are a dark horse of sorts to win the Mountain West.
Then there is Troy Calhoun and Air Force. The Falcons won nine games over the last two seasons, but Calhoun has an 
experienced roster, including quarterback Liam Szarka, who led one of the most efficient offenses in the country last 
season. Air Force could absolutely find a way to the table of contenders by season’s end.
Heartbreak usually leads to change. People hit the gym, change their diets and generally try to improve themselves when 
they get out of a long-term relationship. The Mountain West certainly tried to do that with the additions it made, but it’s 
unlikely those that departed for the Pac-12 are amazed by the changes to the conference. Either way, this should be a 
raucous and volatile season in the Mountain West, and that is great for bettors.
