2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
The Mid-American Conference performed a one-for-one swap this offseason, shipping off Northern Illinois to the 
Mountain West and acquiring Sacramento State from the Big Sky Conference at the FCS level. If you don’t understand 
that trade from a geographic standpoint, neither do I, but the Hornets signed a five-year deal to be a football-only MAC 
member, as the basketball team will play in the Big West.
With UMass rejoining the MAC last season, the conference has 13 teams, with three in Michigan, one in New York, six in 
Ohio, one in Indiana, one in Massachusetts, and then one in California. As usual, realignment remains a head-scratcher, 
but we sure have some fascinating travel spots for this season as a result, especially when we get to MACtion in late 
October and early November with weeknight games.
On the whole, the conference isn’t very strong again, but somebody will have to win it. UMass went 0-12 and 0-8 in 
conference play at one extreme, while Western Michigan went 10-4, including a 7-1 record against MAC opponents. 
The Broncos put an exclamation point on their season with a 10-point win over Miami (OH) in Detroit for the MAC 
Championship and a slaughter of Kennesaw State in the Myrtle Beach Bowl.
WMU (+360) is co-favored to run it back and their company is Miami, so we could be looking at our first MAC 
Championship Game rematch since Northern Illinois and Bowling Green played three straight title games from 2013-15. 
The Falcons (16-1) may not be much of a factor, but their archrival Toledo (4-1) is getting some market love, even with 
former head coach Jason Candle at UConn. Ohio (+650) and Central Michigan (+750) round out the single-digit shots in 
what looks like a wide-open conference again.
Three teams tied for second at 6-2 last season and there was a battle until the end in 2024 with two teams at 7-1 and two at 
6-2. It usually comes down to the last week to see who will represent the conference at Ford Field and this season looks to 
follow that same path.
