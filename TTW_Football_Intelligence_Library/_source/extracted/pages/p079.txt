2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
From a College Football Playoff standpoint, the ACC had grown accustomed to leaving the party early until Miami showed 
up in a big way this past season. Clemson was a major player in the late 2010s, including two National Championships, 
but their one appearance in the last five seasons was a one-and-done as the No. 12 team in 2024. Florida State had a 
cameo appearance back in 2014 and relative newcomer SMU, who joined the conference in 2024, had a very short-lived 
appearance that same season.
The Hurricanes were unable to finish the job and lost to Indiana, but Mario Cristobal was able to flip the proverbial bird 
to the doubters and haters with wins over Texas A&M, Ohio State, and Ole Miss before the 27-21 loss to the Hoosiers. The 
question now is whether or not Miami can piggyback off of that success. Because if they can’t, it looks like another season 
in which the ACC will be represented in the CFP simply by virtue of being a major conference with an automatic bid and 
having a seat at the table tall enough to get a team in the field.
Incredibly, Miami didn’t even play in the ACC Championship Game last season, as that was between Duke and Virginia. 
The Blue Devils upset the Cavaliers, which kept Virginia out of the CFP. In the end, it worked out and the conference’s 
best team made the biggest statement in December and January.
Despite some extremely heavy personnel losses, especially on defense, the Hurricanes (-136) still seem to have the inside 
track in the conference as a very clear odds-on favorite to celebrate in Charlotte on December 5. With SMU (7-1) and 
Louisville (8-1) as the only other teams lined in single digits to win the ACC at time of publish, it sure looks like the door is 
open for somebody to step up and challenge Miami. After all, five teams went 6-2 in conference play and five more went 
4-4 last season. I’ll be nice and call it parity. Really, this just isn’t a very good conference top to bottom.
