2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
The time at the top of the country has seemingly come to an end for the SEC. It has been three years since a member 
of the conference won a national championship. The Big Ten has dominated the last three seasons, all with different 
champions. The conversation all offseason has been about the SEC reclaiming its throne, and there is certainly a strong 
chance the conference can do just that with its depth of championship contenders.
Georgia — the last SEC team to win it all — looks primed to grab its third title of the Kirby Smart era. The Bulldogs have a 
Heisman Trophy contender in quarterback Gunner Stockton and one of the most experienced rosters in the country.
Texas has the experience as well, and the blue-chip prospect at quarterback in Arch Manning. Steve Sarkisian has some 
CFP wins under his belt and is dying to add to that this season. Given the additions the Longhorns made in the portal, as 
well as the returning production throughout the roster, the market believes this team is a true contender.
Then, you get to some of the dark-horse contenders for both a conference title and national championship.
Alabama and Texas A&M both made the CFP last season, but shared similar fates in being dispatched by the eventual 
participants in the national championship game. The Crimson Tide will have to work in a new quarterback, but they do 
have a talented roster with a great defense. So too do the Aggies. The difference between the two is the experience Marcel 
Reed has at quarterback.
Let’s not forget Ole Miss, which finished a drive short of a berth in the final, or Oklahoma, which returns most of the best 
defense in the country.
The expectations for the SEC are always a national championship. This year is no different, and the conference boasts 
many potential contenders. The depth of the conference in terms of quality is not on par with the Big Ten, but no one will 
be shocked if a program from this conference is hoisting a trophy come January.
