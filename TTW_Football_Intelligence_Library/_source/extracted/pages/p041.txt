Here is how the scores are determined:
Returning Head Coach Points
Yes, same Head Coach as 2025: 4 points
No, new Head Coach for 2026: 0 points
Returning Offensive Coordinator Points
Yes, same Offensive Coordinator as 2025: 3 points
No, new Offensive Coordinator for 2026: 0 points
Returning Defensive Coordinator Points
Yes, same Defensive Coordinator as 2025: 3 points
No, new Defensive Coordinator for 2026: 0 points
Same Starting Quarterback Points
Yes, same Starting Quarterback as 2025: 4 points
No, new Starting Quarterback for 2026: 0 points
I reiterate…I consider a transfer quarterback who is expected to start 
for his team a NEW QUARTERBACK, regardless of the level of starting 
experience he brings to the table. For 2026, there are again numerous 
recognizable QBs that have found new starting homes across the country. 
For instance, Josh Hoover at Indiana or Sam Leavitt at LSU are among 
the veteran QBs across the country that boast big-game college football 
experience but will be in new schemes this fall. While they are not your 
typical first-year quarterbacks, they are NEW to those programs.
Total Number of Returning Starters Points
0-7 returning offensive and defensive starters: 0 points
8-9: 1 point
10-12: 2 points
13-16: 3 points
17-19: 4 points
20-22: 5 points
