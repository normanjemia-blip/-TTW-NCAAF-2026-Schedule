Can the offense improve in a hurry under Jacurri?
The Jacurri in question is Jacurri Brown, who spent two years at 
Miami and two years at UCF, but he’s only thrown 148 passes with 
more interceptions (9) than touchdowns (6). What Brown has done, 
though, is effectively run the football. He has 137 carries for 821 
yards, so better than six yards per carry. At Davidson back in 2024, 
Wildcats QBs only had 152 pass attempts in 11 games, as the team 
ran the ball 640 times for 3,720 yards, with QB Andrew Frazier third 
in rush attempts and rush yards. Quinton Jackson was the best 
player on offense for Rice last season with 889 yards and 4.9 yards 
per carry. Last season, the Owls had 647 rush attempts against 224 
pass attempts. I would expect an even starker split this season.
Can the option offense even be successful?
Not all options are created equal, as Navy has recently modified 
theirs and Army just runs theirs like a well-oiled machine, but Rice 
is attempting to do their version of an option in a conference that 
already has two service academies. It makes scheming a lot easier 
for the opposing defense to see a lot of similar core concepts and 
blocking schemes. Rice didn’t face Army last season, but will this 
season. However, they did face Navy and only had 3.1 yards per 
carry. Memphis held them to 2.4 YPC, while UTSA allowed 3.8, UAB 
allowed 3.4, and USF allowed 3.8. Brown could be a difference-
maker as a better runner than Chase Jenkins, but Rice just might 
not be in the best conference for this.
Is everything ‘Kay on defense?
‘Kay as in “okay” under DC Jon Kay, who was a holdover from the 
previous regime. It is a bit surprising that a head coach would 
simply promote the previous head coach’s linebackers coach to 
DC and it didn’t really seem to work out well last season. The Owls 
allowed 6.3 YPP and nearly 33 PPG. It was the worst defensive effort 
for the team since 2022. And, even with a run-heavy approach, the 
team still finished -4 in turnover margin with just seven takeaways. 
Rice was tied for ninth in the nation in turnovers lost and still 
finished with a negative TO margin. The top seven tacklers from last 
season are gone, but starting from scratch probably isn’t a bad thing 
with this defense.
