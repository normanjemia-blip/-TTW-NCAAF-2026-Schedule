What does Simmons have in store for an encore?
So, that’s the obvious first question. Interestingly, the team was 
worse in a few key statistical areas, falling from 5.7 to 5.6 YPP on 
offense and going up from 5.2 to 5.9 YPP on defense. However, 
if you look closer, what FIU did in conference play was very 
impressive. They racked up better than 6.2 YPP and allowed just 
over 5.6. It is always important to parse out the paycheck games for 
teams like this, where games at Penn State and at UConn don’t have 
much merit in the metrics or for the next season. Unfortunately for 
Simmons, his leading QB, RB, WR, tackler, and defensive back are 
all gone, so he and his staff had a lot of work to do in the offseason.
Will Kohl cash in his latest opportunity?
To replace Keyone Jenkins, OC Nick Coleman and Simmons were 
able to wrangle JJ Kohl, who was originally at Iowa State then 
went to Appalachian State, where he had a 12/2 TD/INT ratio over 
six starts and some relief efforts. Kohl was a four-star in the 2023 
class out of Iowa and is a massive human being at 6-foot-7 and 
245 pounds. With a new QB, the loss of leading WR Alex Perry 
is probably less significant, as he was going to have to develop 
chemistry with everybody anyway, but Perry did have 530 more 
receiving yards and 25 more catches than anybody else, which is 
part of the reason why he’s at Illinois now. It wouldn’t be terribly 
surprising to see Kohl in something of a timeshare with Delaware 
transfer Zach Marker or freshman DJ Alexander, with the latter two 
as bigger running threats.
How much of a worry is the secondary?
Mister Clark had 13 pass breakups, primarily playing as the nickel, 
and also had 6.5 tackles for loss. He was a truly dynamic player on 
a defense that didn’t have a lot of them. While the front seven has a 
few remaining pieces, including top sack man Kenton Simmons, the 
secondary is a grab bag of transfers. The projected starting corners 
are from Duke and FAU, while the player replacing Clark’s role is a 
multi-time transfer, most recently from Oklahoma State. The safety 
position looks good with homegrown star Shamir Sterlin and two-
year South Alabama starter Wesley Miller. The FIU QBs combined 
for 13 INT last season, but the defense had 21 takeaways (T-26th) 
and the team was exactly even in TO margin. Impact plays like that 
are going to be necessary again.
