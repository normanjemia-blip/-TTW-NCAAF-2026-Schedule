Is Nico Iamaleava ready to take the next step?
After all the drama surrounding his transfer from Tennessee, 
Iamaleava had a disappointing inaugural campaign in Westwood, 
throwing for just 1,929 yards in 11 starts. However, he did complete 
64.4% of his passes and only threw seven interceptions while 
adding 505 yards and four touchdowns on the ground. Chesney 
brings him some help from James Madison including all-purpose 
running back Wayne Knight (1,373 rush yards, nine TDs; 397 rec 
yards, one TD) to go along with returning backs Jaivian Thomas 
and Anthony Woods, plus three offensive line starters Riley Robell, 
Carter Sweezie (both All-Sun Belt), and JD Rayner. Essentially a 
brand-new receiving corps joins holdover Mikey Matthews (33-348-
2) with Landon Ellis (James Madison), Leland Smith (San Jose State), 
Brian Rowe (South Carolina), Aidan Mizell (Florida), and Semaj 
Morgan (Michigan) entering the fold.
Can a true No. 1 wide receiver emerge?
One of the reasons for Iamaleava’s disappointing passing numbers 
is because the wide receivers did not give much help. Nico is a 
playmaker that is most effective throwing on the run instead of 
standing in the pocket, so receivers must get some separation. 
Rowe, from South Carolina, is the most talented of the group. Ellis 
(624 yards) has familiarity with the offense from his time at James 
Madison. Smith (43-688-3) posted good numbers at San Jose State. 
Can the defensive line get some pressure on opposing 
quarterbacks?
No team in FBS was worse in doing so than UCLA last season, as 
they only registered 10 sacks. Chesney did go into the transfer 
portal and brought in key players like Sun Belt Freshman of the 
Year Sahir West (seven sacks) and Aiden Gobaira (four sacks) from 
James Madison. Oklahoma transfer linebacker Samuel Omosigho 
(50 tackles, two sacks). The talented secondary masked a lot of the 
defensive line’s weaknesses last year and it will be a talented group 
in the back again, but the defensive line must help them out up 
front or the Bruins will give plenty of points just like last year (33.4 
PPG, 125th nationally).
