at Brigham Young, Bronco 
Mendenhall decided to take 
his coaching show on the 
road. Utah State is his third 
stop since leaving Provo in 
2015, and he has had a losing 
record since ditching BYU. 
He went 36-38 in six seasons 
at Virginia, 5-7 in one year at 
New Mexico and 6-7 last year 
with the Aggies. Utah State was 
close to a much better finish 
in Mendenhall’s first season, 
losing 29-26 at UNLV and 25-24 
to Boise State late in the year. 
While the Broncos and Rebels 
played for the Mountain West 
championship, the Aggies 
went on to lose the Idaho 
Potato Bowl. Mendenhall must 
replace a veteran quarterback, 
and he has settled on another 
veteran to lead the team into 
the Pac-12.
