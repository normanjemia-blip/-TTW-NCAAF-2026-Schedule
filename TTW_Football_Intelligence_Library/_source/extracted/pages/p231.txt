Will UMass win a game?!
Let’s get the hardest question out of the way early. I’m going to say 
yes. Sacred Heart and Stonehill are two great options, but they 
also host Ball State and Eastern Michigan this season, two teams 
that they didn’t play last season, and I think either one could 
be beatable, if not both. It is also Year 2 for Harasymiak and his 
assistants, although OC Mike Bajakian won’t be along for the ride, 
as he’s the new QB coach of the Cleveland Browns (I swear, the jokes 
write themselves with that organization). Given that the UMass 
offense had 3.9 yards per play and an 8/9 TD/INT ratio from the QBs, 
something tells me that Bajakian isn’t really a big loss. New OC Max 
Warner, who was an offensive assistant at Toledo, seems like an 
upgrade.
Can UMass right the wrongs of last season?
Well, for starters, three different QBs started games and six 
different players, including two of the QBs, had at least 40 rushing 
attempts. Jacquon Gibson had 63 catches and nobody else had more 
than 27. Fifteen players had at least 20 tackles. Injuries played a 
part, but Harasymiak and his staff were just trying to find dudes 
that could ball pretty much all season long. There are quite a few 
P4 transfers on the roster now between this portal cycle and the 
coaching staff’s first one. One of them is Pop Watson, who may start 
at QB. He’s never really gotten a crack to start, but was a three-star 
recruit and the MaxPreps HS QB of the Year in Massachusetts back 
in 2021, so it’s a homecoming for him and he headlines a much 
better QB room.
Will anybody get to the quarterback?
An absence of negative plays was an enormous issue for the UMass 
defense last season. They only racked up 13 sacks and allowed more 
than five yards per carry, including 5.1 YPC in conference action. 
Only Air Force and UCLA had fewer tackles for loss. Ironically, 
Rutgers was one of the teams with fewer sacks than UMass and 
that’s where Harasymiak and his DC Jared Keyte came from. So, 
maybe it’s just the scheme. Jackson State transfer Joshua Nobles 
had 10 sacks before transferring in, but got hurt before the season 
started. Edge William DePaepe was at Indiana. Linebacker Michael 
Robinson was at Rutgers. This group seems decidedly more 
talented.
