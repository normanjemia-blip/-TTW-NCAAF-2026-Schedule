Can the Trojans provide some protection?
One way to avoid unwanted consequences is to use protection. 
If you want your quarterback to stay healthy, perhaps blocking 
for him makes some sense. I had to do a triple take to make sure 
that 59 wasn’t a typo in the sack department for Troy. It wasn’t. 
The Trojans allowed seven more sacks than the next closest team. 
Tucker Kilcrease even had 116 carries and Goose Crowder had 57, 
so there could have been even more. A couple starters are back 
on the offensive line, not that that’s necessarily a good thing, and 
the transfer portal was heavily-utilized to fill out the rest. I find 
it incredible that Troy was exactly even in turnover margin and 
the QBs had a 20/11 TD/INT ratio while being under duress on 
seemingly every dropback.
Who is QB1?
Kilcrease and Crowder must be really good friends. Or they really 
love the school. Either way, it is really rare to see this sort of 
dynamic in college football nowadays with how easy it is to transfer. 
Both guys sat behind Matthew Caldwell in 2024 and went into 
2025 as 1a and 1b, with them flipping spots depending on the day. 
Crowder won the starting gig, but got hurt in the third game. When 
he came back, Kilcrease still got the start against Old Dominion 
and then he got hurt, as Crowder finished out the season. Based 
on the betting market action when Crowder got hurt and Kilcrease 
took over, the sentiment was that Kilcrease was a clear downgrade. 
There are also quite a few players from East Mississippi CC on this 
roster now, including 6-foot-5 QB Rodge Waldrop. I’m very curious 
to see how this all plays out.
Is improvement coming on both sides of the ball?
New OC Adam Austin led a Tarleton offense that had five 50-burgers 
last season and was the highest PPG team at the FCS level and 
second to North Texas in all of Division I. DC Dontae Wright led 
some nice year over year improvements, going from 28.4 PPG in 
2024 to 23.9 PPG last season, plus the team shaved more than 6% 
off of their completion percentage against, had 18 more sacks, and 
allowed 0.4 fewer yards per play. Unfortunately, pretty much all of 
his top defensive players are gone. One who isn’t is Donnie Smith, 
who had 11 sacks. Based on the improvement from 2024 to 2025, I’m 
convinced this staff is adept at finding talent in the portal.
