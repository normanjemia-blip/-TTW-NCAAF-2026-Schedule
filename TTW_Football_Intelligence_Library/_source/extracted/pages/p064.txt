Last season was a transitional 
one for Rice, as Scott Abell 
embraced one of the most 
challenging and difficult jobs 
in FBS after making the leap 
from FCS Davidson. Rice has 
an enrollment under 4,000, 
strict academic requirements, 
and hasn’t had a winning 
season since 2014. You would 
think that being in Houston 
with all of the Texas high 
school football talent, it would 
be easier to put together a 
competent roster, even with 
the low acceptance rate, but it 
hasn’t been.
Even last season, Rice was 
granted a bowl game because 
others backed out and the 
school’s Academic Progress 
Rate (APR) scores were worthy 
of an opportunity, but the 
team only had a 5-7 regular 
season. They only scored 19.1 
PPG and dropped by nearly a 
full yard per play on offense, 
while giving up 1.3 more YPP 
on defense. It’s a tough gig 
and it looks like another tough 
year, but Abell seems up for 
the challenge and brought in a 
decent number of P4 transfers.
