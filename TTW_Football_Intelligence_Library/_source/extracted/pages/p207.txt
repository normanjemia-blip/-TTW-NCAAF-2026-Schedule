How much will Rodney Tisdale improve?
Last season, QB Maverick McIvor and OC Rick Bowie were a 
package deal from Abilene Christian. McIvor did complete 67% 
of his passes, but had just a 12/7 TD/INT ratio, and wasn’t much 
of a runner, though the Hilltoppers did go up nearly 4.5 PPG year 
over year. Bowie was a one-and-done, so Reeder now has the keys 
to the offense and Tisdale, who took over for McIvor, is the man 
in the spotlight. He completed just under 66% of his passes with a 
9/7 TD/INT ratio and had 2.1 yards per carry. The numbers weren’t 
great, but he was a true freshman and, while he has to learn a new 
playbook, he’s the most dynamic option on the roster. Florida State 
transfer Brock Glenn has a chance at a fresh start here if Tisdale 
falters or gets hurt.
How long does it take the offense to gel?
Not only are there questions about Tisdale and Reeder, but KD 
Hutchinson is the only returnee of consequence at the skill 
positions. Tulsa transfer Ajay Allen will likely lead the by-committee 
approach at RB that had decent returns last season. Georgia Tech 
transfer Zion Taylor is probably the most notable WR added to 
the mix, but Jyzaiah Rockwell was a 1,100-yard receiver at the FCS 
ranks last season. It should be noted that the offensive line, while 
a hodgepodge of transfers, has a lot more experience and size than 
last season’s group, including an Alabama transfer. If Tisdale can 
distribute the ball without turning it over and prove to be more of a 
running threat, this unit will be cooking with gas quickly.
Is the defense at risk of regressing?
Jaylen Lewis and Dave Herrard are really the only two returning 
contributors on defense, as DC Davis Merritt had a lot of scouting 
work to do. One silver lining is that only three players had 70+ 
tackles last season, as the Hilltoppers rotated a ton of guys in and 
out to try and stay fresh. Some of last season’s backups will elevate 
to starting roles and that means that they’ve been in the system 
and are familiar with the scheme. Merritt has been on the staff for 
the last three years, so everything is pretty ingrained with those 
who are back in the fold. Last year’s defense only had 19 sacks, 
though, but still allowed just 22.6 PPG, the fewest since 2019 when 
the Hilltoppers allowed just 20.1 PPG and just 3.7 yards per carry. 
Stopping the run was an issue last season that needs to be fixed this 
season and the low sack numbers and complete turnover with the 
front seven all have me pessimistic about the defense.
