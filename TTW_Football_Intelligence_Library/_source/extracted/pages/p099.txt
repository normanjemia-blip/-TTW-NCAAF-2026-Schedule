Does Billy Edwards start all season long? Or at all?
Edwards committed to Wake Forest in 2020 and enrolled in 2021. 
Since then, he’s been at Maryland, Wisconsin, and now North 
Carolina, as he only started two games last season and didn’t really 
look the part before suffering a season-ending injury. It was said 
that Wisconsin paid about $1 million in total NIL compensation to 
Edwards. Perhaps UNC got a deal given that he needed a medical 
waiver to be eligible for this season and did virtually nothing last 
season. Would UNC be better off with 6-foot-5 Miles O’Neill or 
6-foot-4 freshman Travis Burgess? O’Neill was a Petrino recruit to 
Texas A&M and Burgess is a dual-threat four-star and the No. 9 QB 
in the class for 247. It sure seems like the Tar Heels should bet on 
upside and not on Edwards, who was a mediocre starter for the 
Terps two seasons ago.
Did the Tar Heels prioritize the right players?
The QB position looks to be in flux, but the Tar Heels kept their 
leading rusher, leading receiver, and leading sack man even though 
there was a mass exodus to the transfer portal. It sure seems like 
Belichick and former VSiN personality Michael Lombardi isolated 
the in-house players they were willing to pay to stay and those they 
were willing to pay to come to Chapel Bill. RB Demon June only 
had 464 yards, as the team only ran for 3.7 yards per carry, but he 
was clearly the best of the backs. WR Jordan Shipp easily paced 
the team with 60 receptions for 671 yards and June was actually 
third in receiving yards. Melkart Abou-Jaoude had 10.5 sacks. Kaleb 
Cost and Jaiden Patterson were also holdovers at defensive back. 
It’s a solid core. Now we’ll see how the coaches and recruiting 
coordinators did to bolster it.
What is the perception of the program?
There was certainly a lot of coverage early about North Carolina 
football with the hiring of Belichick and all of the off-the-field 
stuff with his girlfriend and others around the program. From the 
outside, the optics weren’t great. However, the Tar Heels pulled in 
the 19th-ranked recruiting class of incoming freshmen and over 40 
recruits, so whatever the school is selling, some players certainly 
seem to be buying. Only West Virginia got more class of 2026 
commits. We know Belichick can identify talent and the players that 
fit what he wants to do. A case could be made that there’s a lot of 
hope this season.
