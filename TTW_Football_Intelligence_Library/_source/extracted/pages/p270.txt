When projecting college football 
teams, it’s no secret the two 
most important positions of 
handicapping emphasis are the 
head coach and quarterback. 
Fresno State certainly has a 
winning coach. Matt Entz is 
about as good as it gets. Entz, 53, 
coached North Dakota State to 
two FCS national championships 
in five years and exited with a 
record of 60-11. In 2025, he took 
over a Fresno State team that 
went 6-7 the previous season 
and guided the Bulldogs to a 
9-4 finish punctuated by a win 
in the Arizona Bowl. He’s got a 
new quarterback this season, 
and that position appears to 
be upgraded, so beware of the 
Bulldogs. Entz could be lifting 
Fresno to the top of the Pac-12.
