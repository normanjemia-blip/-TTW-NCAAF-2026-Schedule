Who takes over for Ty Simpson at quarterback?
The expectation is that redshirt freshman Keelon Russell will 
be the starter for Alabama this season, but DeBoer will have a 
“competition” for the job when fall camp begins. Russell adds an 
athletic dynamic to the position that Simpson did not, and it is one 
of many reasons why many expect him to win the job. Russell also 
fits the RPO offense that DeBoer and offensive coordinator Ryan 
Grubb want to run. The Tide finished 31st in EPA per play through 
the air and 21st in success rate when dropping back. Whether it is 
Russell or senior Austin Mack, both will likely be less efficient than 
Simpson was a season ago. Bettors can expect more production on 
the ground from the position, though.
Can Alabama find its rushing attack once again?
There was an immense load on the passing attack’s shoulders last 
year because Alabama could not run the ball. The Crimson Tide 
averaged just 3.4 yards per carry and 104 rush yards per game. 
They finished 132nd in rush success rate (34.5%), placed in the 
18th percentile in rush EPA per play and in the sixth percentile in 
explosive run rate. Leading rusher Jam Miller (520 yards) is gone, 
and so too are six offensive linemen who started games. Sophomore 
Michael Carroll — who will start at either right guard or tackle — is 
the lone returnee. Perhaps that is a good thing for such an anemic 
rush offense. Either way, finding some semblance of balance with 
this unit needs to be a priority this season.
Is this team a national championship contender?
If Russell wins the job and is everything most expect, the answer is 
yes. Many of the questions with the Tide are on the offensive side of 
the ball. On defense, seven starters are back from one of the better 
units in college football. The Tide finished 29th in opponent EPA 
per play and 25th in success rate. In fact, the defense got somewhat 
unlucky last season, ranking in the 91st percentile in early-down 
EPA per play but the 51st percentile in late-down success rate. 
Alabama’s secondary is one of the best in the country, with three 
starters back and five-star freshman Jorden Edmonds now in the 
mix. The Tide have what they need on defense. If the offense hits, 
this team should be able to romp through a manageable schedule 
and into the College Football Playoff.
