Does Arch Manning pick up where he left off?
There were many who were disappointed by the season Manning 
put together, but over the last six games he looked every bit of the 
blue-chip prospect he was lauded to be. In those contests, Manning 
threw 14 touchdowns with a turnover-worthy play rate of just 
1.2%. He averaged 7.8 yards per attempt and added another five 
touchdowns on the ground. Texas went 5-1 SU/3-2-1 ATS in those 
games. For the season, Manning was the eighth-best passer in the 
conference by PFF standards and barely completed 60% of his 
passes. But if he can pick up on the form he had by season’s end, 
this offense could be more explosive.
Will the offensive line improve in both facets?
In part, Manning struggled due to the pressure he faced. Manning 
was pressured on 35.6% of his dropbacks last season. His 165 
dropbacks under pressure were the most in the SEC. In the running 
game, the issues were clear. Texas averaged 4.2 yards per carry and 
138 yards per game in 2025. In order to fix the issues, Sarkisian 
helped his group by hitting the portal, bringing in a few bodies, but 
right tackle Melvin Siani from Wake Forest was the crown jewel. 
Couple that with the return of left tackle Trevor Goosby and center 
Connor Robertson, and this unit should be able to provide better 
protection for Manning while also giving more push in the run 
game.
Can the secondary be a bigger strength?
During SEC media days, the Longhorns got a big win when five-star 
cornerback — and ESPN’s top-ranked 2027 corner — John Meredith 
III reclassified into the 2026 cycle in order to enroll for the fall. 
It’s unknown what role he will play, but a talent like that is never 
a negative, especially for a unit looking for more. Texas was 66th 
in defensive success rate against the pass last season and 33rd in 
opponent EPA per play through the air. Jelani McDonald (80 tackles, 
3 pass breakups, 3 interceptions) is back at safety, but the defensive 
backfield lost three starters. Will Muschamp was also hired to run 
this defense in the offseason. There should be some improvement 
with this group overall.
