Can Manny Rojas put the ‘D’ back in ‘elaware?
Even in a lackluster conference, the leap from FCS to FBS was 
on display on the defensive side for the Blue Hens. In their final 
season as a FCS member, they allowed 21 PPG and 5.3 YPP. In their 
first season at the FBS level, they allowed 31 PPG and 6.0 YPP. The 
biggest disappointment - or maybe most noticeable development - is 
that opponents ran for 4.6 yards per carry, including 4.5 YPC from 
Conference USA foes. Rojas has been with Delaware since 2020, so 
even before Carty took over. He kept him as the DC, but you wonder 
about his allegiance to him if the defense falters again in Year 2, 
especially with three of their four leading tacklers back and a few 
FBS-level transfers.
Will the offensive line be the team’s downfall?
Last season, the Blue Hens only rushed for 3.8 YPC and a lot of 
that was because Minicucci had 112 carries for just 235 yards. His 
average of 2.1 YPC dragged down the team as a whole, though 
second-leading rusher Viron Ellison only had 3.9 YPC himself. 
While the offensive line technically only gave up 27 sacks, and 
just 14 in conference play, it was pretty clear that Minicucci was 
the reason why with scrambles and some designed short-yardage 
runs. Sadly, the Blue Hens lost two long-term OL starters in Patrick 
Shupp and Fintan Brose, though both tackles combined for 24 starts 
at various positions and the center is back as well. This was not 
an area flush with transfers for Delaware, so they’ll have to hope 
internal options get better.
It’s a game of adjustments, right?
Delaware played some tight games in league play, losing by a field 
goal to both Western Kentucky and Sam Houston State, while 
beating Middle Tennessee by three and Louisiana Tech by one. Coin 
flips like that will likely determine the outcome for the season, but 
those are more established programs at the FBS level with stronger 
recruiting pipelines. Delaware’s talent level doesn’t seem that much 
higher. They did not play Kennesaw State, Missouri State, or New 
Mexico State last season, but they will this season, with the NMSU 
game all the way out in Las Cruces on November 21. Kennesaw 
State won the conference. The others all have film on them now. 
How will Carty and his crew adjust?
