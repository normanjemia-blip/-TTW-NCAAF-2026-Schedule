2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
The Big Ten is aiming for its fourth consecutive CFP National Championship here in 2026. In 2023, Michigan won its first national title 
since 1997 before Jim Harbaugh left his alma mater and took his NCAA show-cause penalty to return to coaching in the NFL. In 2024, 
the Wolverines’ archrival Ohio State, led by the most talented roster in college football, removed the critics from Ryan Day’s back and 
won its first national title since 2014. Well, the Big Ten “three-peated” last season. So, was it Ohio State again? Michigan? Penn State? 
Oregon? USC? No, it was the Indiana Hoosiers going 16-0 and becoming the first FBS team since Yale in 1894 to go 16-0. 
Curt Cignetti and his Hoosiers also won their first outright Big Ten Championship since 1945. Indiana’s rise to national prominence 
not only included these championships but was also home to its first Heisman Trophy Winner Fernando Mendoza. Indiana (+250) will 
attempt to defend with heavy retention on the offensive line and defense to go along with a portal overhaul that includes TCU transfer 
QB Josh Hoover to replace Mendoza. 
Ohio State (+180), defeated by the Hoosiers in the Big Ten Championship Game, returns QB Julian Sayin and WR Jeremiah Smith as 
perhaps the best QB-WR tandem in college football. Oregon (+260), who lost to the Hoosiers twice, brings back 14 starters including QB 
Dante Moore and arguably the nation’s best defensive line. 
Those three teams will be consensus top five nationally to start the season, but USC (14-1) is looking to get to the top of the Big Ten for 
the first time with a defense led by former TCU coach Gary Patterson, who was lured out of retirement. 
Four programs have new coaches including Michigan (15-1), who brings in Kyle Whittingham from Utah to restore its tarnished image 
from the Harbaugh recruiting violations and the Sherrone Moore scandal; Michigan State (500-1), who turns to former Northwestern 
head coach Pat Fitzgerald; Penn State (20-1), who brings in Matt Campbell from Iowa State to win the big ones that James Franklin 
seemingly never could; UCLA (160-1), who figured that if Indiana can hire a successful head coach from James Madison, then why not 
do the same with Bob Chesney?
