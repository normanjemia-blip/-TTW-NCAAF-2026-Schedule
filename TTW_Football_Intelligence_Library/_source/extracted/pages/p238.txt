For the first time since 2016, the 
Rockets will not be playing for 
Jason Candle. Hell, for the first 
time since 2009, Candle isn’t even 
with the program. He’s now the 
head coach at UConn and the 
Rockets moved quickly to replace 
him with Mike Jacobs, who spent 
the last two seasons with Mercer 
at the FCS level. Jacobs is a 
northwest Ohio native and his staff 
also consists of people with Ohio 
ties, including former Tiffin and 
Ohio Dominican head coach OC 
Cris Reisert and Cleveland native 
and Youngstown State DC Jahmal 
Brown.
Jacobs was a long snapper and 
offensive lineman at Ohio State 
from 1997-2001 and got into 
coaching pretty much right after 
that. His first head coaching gig 
was at Notre Dame College about 
a half hour from downtown 
Cleveland. This is something of 
a step up for the 47-year-old, but 
he took Mercer to the playoffs in 
back-to-back seasons and may very 
well be an upgrade over Candle, 
something hard to say about a guy 
who won 65% of his games overall 
and 68% of conference games.

The schedule is pretty soft for 
Jacobs in Year 1, as the Rockets 
don’t face Miami of Ohio, get 
reigning champ Western Michigan 
at home, and get a visit from San 
Diego State in non-conference 
action. It will take time for things 
to come together and the opener 
against Michigan State may not 
be pretty, especially for a defense 
that allowed just 13.3 PPG and 
96 rushing yards per game last 
season, but Jacobs seems like 
a good hire and there’s at least 
some familiarity thanks to all the 
transfers from a team that finished 
in the FCS Top 25 each of the last 
two seasons. My projection is 8.04 
wins.
