Is Demond Williams Jr. all-in?
NIL and transfer portal are the way of the world in college football 
today. However, this got perilously close to Williams leaving and 
lawyers were about to get involved. Williams got some bad advice 
from his representatives and perhaps all of that is behind him. 
Can everyone let bygones be bygones? If so, Williams is one of the 
more dynamic quarterbacks in the conference. He led the Huskies 
last season to top-five marks in both scoring offense (No. 4, 34.1 
PPG) and total offense (No. 5, 409.9 YPG), but this time around he 
must do it without his best players from last season including wide 
receiver Denzel Boston and running back Jonah Coleman. Receiver 
Dezmen Roebuck (42-560-7) and tight end Decker DeGraaf (32-360-
2) are back and four-star receivers Jordan Clay, Mason James, and 
Trez Davis are part of Washington’s highest-rated recruiting class in 
school history. 
Who emerges at running back?
Jonah Coleman is off to the NFL and his heir apparent Adam 
Mohammed transferred to Cal. They added seniors Jayden Limar 
(Oregon) and Trey Cooley (Troy) out of the portal, but both missed 
the spring with injuries and Jordan Washington (27-233-1) was also 
injured in spring ball, so redshirt freshman Quaid Carr and true 
freshman Brian Bonner got most of the reps. Whoever emerges in 
the backfield should have a very good offensive line to run behind 
as four starters return including the Hatchett brothers – center 
Landen and right guard Geirean – plus five-star freshman Kodi 
Greene should start at left tackle. 
Can the Huskies beat anyone good?
Washington has shown the ability to beat the teams that they are 
supposed to beat, but this is a program still looking for a signature 
victory to get the Husky faithful to fully believe in Fisch. The 
Huskies are just 2-7 in Big Ten road games under Fisch with the two 
victories coming last year at Maryland and at UCLA. This year there 
are tests at USC, at Oregon, and at Nebraska and hosting Indiana in 
the home finale. Much will depend on Williams, who had just 645 
yards with three touchdowns and six interceptions in Washington’s 
four defeats.
