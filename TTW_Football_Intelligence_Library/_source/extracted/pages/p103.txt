Has SMU done enough to reload on offense?
Jennings posted a 26/13 TD/INT ratio last season and did virtually 
nothing as a runner, which was the biggest difference from 2024 
to 2025. Jennings wasn’t a prolific runner by any means, but he 
had 300 more rushing yards in ‘24 compared to ‘25. As a guy with 
double-digit interceptions in each of the last two seasons, offsetting 
those with production in other ways is important. But, he heads 
into this season without his two top receivers in yardage from last 
season and four of the top five, plus leading rushers TJ Harden and 
Chris Johnson both moved on. SMU went portal shopping for RB 
Kendrick Raphael, who is at his third ACC school now, and some 
help at WR and TE. Lashlee is his own OC, but did lose right-hand 
man Casey Woods to the HC job at Missouri State. As a reminder of 
how old we’re all getting, D’Eriq King is one of the co-OCs.
Is the defense the bigger worry?
The Mustangs have surpassed six yards per play on offense in each 
of Lashlee’s four seasons at the helm and last year’s 32.2 PPG were 
the lowest in that span. But, the defense has been the separator 
from Lashlee’s first year record of 7-6 to the 31-10 over the last 
three seasons. Last year’s unit was 0.5 YPP worse than the previous 
one, but allowed 1.6 fewer PPG. Over the last three seasons, SMU 
has racked up 125 sacks, and last season, even with Jennings’ 
interception issue, the Mustangs were +9 in TO margin with 28 
takeaways. Former DC Scott Symons is still in Dallas, but now with 
the Cowboys. Lashlee kept it in-house and promoted from within, 
but five of the top seven tackles are gone, including leading sack 
man Isaiah Smith. Per 247Sports, SMU brought in the sixth-ranked 
transfer portal class in the 17-team ACC with a lot of needs at impact 
positions on both sides of the ball.
Let’s talk more about sacks, shall we?
Some handicappers I respect greatly look at sack differential as 
a major tenet of their handicapping. SMU was +16 last season, a 
decrease from +25 the season prior and +31 the year before that. 
While Jennings has had some issues taking care of the football, 
this is a pass-first offense and he has only been sacked 33 times on 
well over 800 dropbacks. While he’s not an explosive runner, he has 
171 carries over the past two seasons that have turned potential 
negative plays into slightly positive or at least not completely 
detrimental plays. On the other side, it will be interesting to see if 
new DC Maurice Crum and holdover co-DC Ricky Hunley Jr. can 
keep up the pressure on defense, considering all four Mustangs 
with at least five sacks have moved on.
