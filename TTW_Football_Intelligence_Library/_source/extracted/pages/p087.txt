Who does what Darian did?
Mensah was really good for Duke after transferring in from Tulane 
and now he’s leveled up again as the expected starting QB for, 
ironically, Miami. He completed over two-thirds of his passes and 
nearly passed for 4,000 yards while leading the conference in pass 
attempts, passing yards, and passing touchdowns. Eget played 
extensively with the Spartans and had over 5,500 yards, but only 
30 TD against 19 INT. It might be up to redshirt freshman Dan 
Mahan, who got all the spring opportunities he could handle with 
Eget hurt. OC Jonathan Brewer saw his group improve by a full 
yard per play and 8.3 PPG in Year 2, but he’s mostly starting from 
scratch in Year 3.
Should the show run through Sheppard?
Nate Sheppard ran for 1,132 yards and 11 TD with 5.7 yards per 
pop as a true freshman last season. Despite the loss of an NFL-
caliber lineman in the April draft, the Blue Devils are still big and 
experienced up front. While Eget or Mahan have an intriguing 
transfer in Penn’s 1,000-yard WR Jared Richardson, their other 
two primary receivers for this season look like 5-foot-8 Jayden 
Moore and 5-foot-9 Javen Nicholas, a Charlotte transfer. While 
Duke’s offensive numbers improved, the defensive numbers got 
significantly worse in Diaz’s second year, so a ground-and-pound 
type of approach might be a good way to go at least early on, 
perhaps leading to some value on Unders.
Can Manny find the manpower on defense?
As an offense, Duke had 6.2 YPP, 285 passing yards per game, 
and a 66.4% completion percentage. Opponents had 6.3 YPP, 277 
passing yards per game, and a 67.2% completion rate. In other 
words, the Blue Devils were rather fortunate that Mensah played 
as well as he did and that the team finished +10 in TO margin. 
Duke was comfortably outgained on a per-play basis in ACC 
action. The Blue Devils also went from 43 sacks down to 26. Across 
the board, the defensive numbers were worse, including a 1.3-yard 
spike in YPP allowed that is hard to overlook given the offensive 
losses. With Diaz so accomplished on the defensive side, it’s time 
for that unit to step up.
