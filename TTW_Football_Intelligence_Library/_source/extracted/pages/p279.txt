How different will the offense look with a new quarterback?
Bryson Barnes was a dual-threat weapon for the Aggies while 
leading the team in rushing (740 yards and 10 touchdowns) and 
passing for 2,803 yards and 18 touchdowns. McCae Hillstead, a 
backup at BYU a year ago, won Utah State’s starting job in the 
spring. Hillstead is a familiar face because he actually started four 
games for the Aggies in 2023. He’s also a running threat. Offensive 
coordinator Robert Anae is known for aerial attacks, but the ground 
game will be emphasized more with senior Javen Jacobs returning 
as the featured back behind Hillstead. The interior line is manned 
by veterans and appears to be solid. The offense was potent (30.9 
PPG) with Barnes running it and the numbers will likely decline a 
little. Hillstead obviously has big shoes to fill.
Will Bronco build a better defense?
Utah State allowed 28.7 PPG last season — surrendering 55 points in 
a loss at Vanderbilt and 44 each in losses at Texas A&M and Hawaii. 
There were positive signs late in the season, however, and one was 
in Mendenhall’s best win, 28-17 at Fresno State. Two of the top three 
tacklers are returning, including senior safety Brevin Hamblin 
(114 tackles). The leader of this defense could be well-traveled 
linebacker Harrison Taggart, who previously played at Oregon, 
BYU and California. Mendenhall was known for producing strong 
defenses at BYU, but his reputation on that side of the ball has faded 
the past few years. The Aggies will be better defensively, but it’s 
tough to say how much better.
Is the road schedule too challenging to overcome?
The Aggies should be 2-2 after four games, assuming they handle 
home games against Idaho State and Troy. The major problems 
Mendenhall faces will be away from home because Utah State plays 
at Washington and Utah in the first month in addition to Pac-12 
road games against Boise State, San Diego State and Texas State. 
Utah State has a 1-15 record against Utah in the Beehive Boot rivalry 
since 1998. The road schedule stacks the odds against the Aggies 
returning to a bowl.
