Holy Toledo, who are these guys?
There are virtually no recognizable names on the roster for the 
Rockets this season. Returning production is hard to find, as the top 
returnee in tackles had 35 and the top returnee in yards of any kind 
had 194 receiving yards. It’s hardly a surprise, as Candle left when 
a lot of his players were running out of eligibility and a head coach 
leaving means that the transfer portal is wide open. 247Sports lists 
37 transfers for the Rockets, who have the third-best MAC class per 
their ranking. John Alan Richter is not one of those transfers, so he’s 
a name that Rockets fans may recognize. Otherwise, there are a lot 
of Mercer transfers who were with Jacobs, including RB CJ Miller 
and WR Adjatay Dabbs, plus there are some faces on the OL familiar 
to Reisert, as they came with him from Gardner-Webb.
What kind of offense should we expect to see?
Under Jacobs, Mercer racked up 6.8 yards per play last season with 
a balanced approach that left them third in the nation in yards per 
game. The Bears had 4.9 yards per carry on more than 400 rushing 
attempts and threw for over 3,800 yards. Toledo may have been a 
little more run-heavy last season, but that’s also because they had 
a lot of leads to protect and some big leads at that. Obviously this 
year’s team won’t have the same defense as last year’s squad, so we’ll 
have to wait and see how the game state plays out. With Miller, Penn 
State transfer Cory Smith, and two senior tackles who played for the 
Rockets last season, I would expect them to lean on the run at the 
outset.
What kind of defense should we expect to see?
This is going to be an aggressive defense under Brown. Last year’s 
Mercer group only allowed 3.4 yards per carry, but gave up 5.7 
yards per play because they got burned for some explosives in the 
passing game. The Bears had 42 sacks in 12 games, but also only 
generated 15 takeaways. With 96 tackles for loss, you can see how 
they pursued the run heavily and also got to the quarterback, but an 
evasive QB had the chance to burn them. There are some running 
QBs in the MAC, but not as many this season as we’ve seen in other 
years. With quite a few Mercer transfers who know the scheme, this 
should be an exciting defense to watch.
