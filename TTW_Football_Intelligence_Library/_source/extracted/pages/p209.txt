2026 VSiN COLLEGE FOOTBALL BETTING GUIDE
Notre Dame and UConn are on two very different paths this season. With the new College Football Playoff qualification 
and seeding rules, all the Fighting Irish have to do is finish in the top 12 in the CFP rankings and they will automatically 
be in the field. Based on how good Notre Dame looks this season, it would be a major upset if they aren’t in the field. In 
fact, DraftKings had the Fighting Irish at -800 to Make the CFP and the ‘No’ was 5-1 for anybody brave enough to take that 
chance.
While UConn never has CFP aspirations, the Huskies were at least worthy of being mentioned in the same breath as the 
Fighting Irish with back-to-back 9-4 seasons. Unfortunately, it does look like the tide will turn in Storrs, at least to some 
degree, with Jim Mora Jr. now at Colorado State. Many believe that Jason Candle is a good hire, but he’s had a knack for 
losing games as a favorite over the course of his career at Toledo and the Huskies have basically nobody back from last 
season’s team.
The story is different in South Bend. While star RBs Jeremiyah Love and Jadarian Price are now in the NFL, QB CJ Carr 
is among the Heisman favorites and the defense is chock full of returning production. After missing out on the CFP last 
season and after losing to Ohio State in the CFP Championship the previous season, there may not be a more motivated 
team than the Fighting Irish and the schedule has absolutely softened up after last season’s early losses to Miami and 
Texas A&M proved too much to overcome in the rankings.
