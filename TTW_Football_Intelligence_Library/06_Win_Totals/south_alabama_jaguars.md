## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen opens by admitting he cannot always explain why a recruiting jump happens, then records the jump: in his third season, coming off 4-8, Major Applewhite secured his best recruit and transfer class, ranked 86th — 31 positions better than the prior year — with several Power Four transfers among the newcomers. Quarterback Bishop Davenport returns for his senior season after leading the team to 26.5 points per game. He adds that the Sun Belt has few teams contending to be favorite and that the Jaguars' conference slate is mild at best, and concludes he likes them to return to a bowl.
