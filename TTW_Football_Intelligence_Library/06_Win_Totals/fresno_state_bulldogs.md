## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen records that Fresno State exceeded his expectations in Matt Entz's first year, finishing 9-4 where he would have allowed a coach time to find his footing. Entz followed it with a recruiting class 247Sports graded as extremely improved. With 13 starters back the Bulldogs also qualify for one of his transition systems: of 141 teams over five years that brought back at least four more starters than the prior season, only 40 got worse, and the rest averaged a 15% winning-percentage gain — about +3.2 wins — with a 6.7% ATS rise.
