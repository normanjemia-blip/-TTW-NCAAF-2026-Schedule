## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen explains the 10-3 to 3-9 fall as the standard yo-yo — heavy losses from a successful team, compounded by turnovers — and then applies his nowhere-to-go-but-up system: since 2021, of 21 teams winning four or fewer games with a turnover ratio of -0.8 or worse, only two regressed, and the other 19 averaged +2.3 wins and a 15.3% straight-up improvement. He adds recruiting: Fran Brown, in his third season, landed the second-highest-rated class Syracuse has seen in 17 years despite the 3-9 record, which Makinen reads as evidence he is a strong salesman. With quarterback Steve Angeli back as a senior and the ACC looking down, he expects a rebound.
