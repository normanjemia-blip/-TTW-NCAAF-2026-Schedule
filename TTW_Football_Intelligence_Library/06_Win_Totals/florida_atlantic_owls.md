## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen's case is regression-to-the-mean stacked three ways. Zach Kittley's first FAU team led the country in offensive plays and passing yards per game and also led it in giveaways. He records quarterback Caden Veltkamp back for his senior year after 3,600-plus passing yards, plus 11 other starters, and then lists three system angles the Owls qualify for: teams going .500 or better ATS despite a turnover margin of -0.8 or worse (only four of 18 regressed, average +1.4 wins); teams winning four or fewer games with that same turnover ratio since 2021 (only two of 21 regressed, average +2.3 wins); and teams adding at least four starters year over year (only 40 of 141 got worse).
