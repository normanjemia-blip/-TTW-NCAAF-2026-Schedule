## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen's case is a new-coach-plus-returning-quarterback system. Bob Chesney arrives from a James Madison CFP season to replace DeShaun Foster, who went 8-16 over two years. He records 2025 as a massive disappointment, particularly after landing Nico Iamaleava, who he says was vilified when the team struggled and praised during a shocking three-game Big Ten winning streak in October. Chesney gets Iamaleava back plus numerous Power Four transfers, amounting to 18 starting players filling spots. The system: over five years, 21 teams that won under 50% and then brought back their starting quarterback while hiring a new head coach and coordinators improved by 15.2% straight up (+2.1 wins) and 13.3% ATS, with only one getting worse. He counts six very winnable home games and calls the coaching upgrade obvious.
