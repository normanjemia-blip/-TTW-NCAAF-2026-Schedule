## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen records recruiting dropping off severely at San Jose State without third-year head coach Ken Niumatalolo reviving it, and says last season's 3-9 is where the results finally showed. He judges the incoming talent insufficient to change 2026 and says the coaching magic from Navy has worn off. Only three starters return from the 3-9 team, triggering his returning-starters system: of 51 teams over five years with at least eight fewer returning starters, only 15 improved, and the rest fell 24% and -3.3 wins. He adds an offensive coordinator change combined with little returning experience, where of 56 teams hiring a new coordinator with four or fewer offensive starters and a new quarterback, only 19 improved and the other 37 fell -2.4 wins while going 45.1% ATS — a group he calls a strong play-against.
