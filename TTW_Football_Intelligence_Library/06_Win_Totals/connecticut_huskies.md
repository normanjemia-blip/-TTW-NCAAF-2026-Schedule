## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen is complimentary about the hire and pessimistic about the year. Jason Candle went 81-44 over a decade at Toledo with every season at .500 or better, and inherits a UConn coming off back-to-back nine-win seasons under Jim Mora Jr. But he will have one of the most inexperienced rosters in the country and a tougher schedule than UConn has been used to, with three Power Four opponents and several prominent Group of Five programmes. Makinen notes the oddsmakers already expect a sharp drop-off at 5.5. He then stacks three systems: teams off a 60%-plus season with four or fewer offensive starters back and a new quarterback drop -3.7 wins on average; the eight of those that also added a new head coach fell 29% and -4.4 wins; and 27 teams over a decade coming off a +1.0 turnover differential while starting fresh at quarterback dropped 2.6 wins.
