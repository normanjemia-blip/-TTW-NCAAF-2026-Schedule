## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen says he has always been puzzled that Jamey Chadwell left Coastal Carolina for Liberty, believing recruiting would naturally be tougher in Lynchburg. A solid 2025 class produced a 4-8 record, and the 2026 class fell by 34 ranking positions. He is not sure the worst is behind the Flames and is openly intrigued that oddsmakers posted a significantly improved 8.5 total after those struggles, and that respected prognosticators put Liberty atop Conference USA. He adds a schedule point: after three straight years with the nation's easiest schedule on paper, Liberty's is now sixth-easiest. His closing question is the argument — the team might be better than last year, but five games better seems a stretch.
