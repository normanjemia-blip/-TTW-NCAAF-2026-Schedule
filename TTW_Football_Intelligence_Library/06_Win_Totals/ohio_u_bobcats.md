## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen notes Ohio is undergoing massive change for first-year head coach John Hauser, while conceding less than most on this list since Hauser was previously the defensive coordinator. He applies the new-coach-plus-new-quarterback system — teams off a 60%-plus season with four or fewer offensive starters back and a new quarterback drop -3.7 wins, and the eight that also changed head coach fell 29% and -4.4 wins — then does the arithmetic openly: a 4.4-win drop would put Ohio at 3.6-8.4, which he calls a bit drastic given a decade of consistency, while still expecting a drop. His second angle is luck: five of nine wins came by seven points or fewer, and of 73 teams over 13 seasons returning 14 or fewer starters with a new quarterback after four or more such wins, only 11 improved.
