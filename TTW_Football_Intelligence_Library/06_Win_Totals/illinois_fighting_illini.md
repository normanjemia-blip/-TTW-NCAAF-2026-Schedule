## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen returns to a bet that won for him last season. His case is programme trajectory: Bret Bielema's best recruiting class on record, 19 wins over two seasons, and a rise from Big Ten recruiting bottom-feeder to a mid-level destination — which he reads as a corner turned rather than a spike. He grants the loss of Luke Altmyer but replaces him with another former Big Ten starter in Katin Houser from Michigan State, with 11 other starters in place. On the schedule he names Ohio State and Oregon and says it is hard to see the Illini as a likely loser in any other game.
