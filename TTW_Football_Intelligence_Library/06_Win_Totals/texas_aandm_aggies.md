## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen makes this his stability showcase. Mike Elko's third year follows a CFP appearance ended by Miami at home, and he expects last season's three-win growth to be a stepping stone to elite territory. Quarterback Marcel Reed returns as a junior alongside 17 other starters. Three angles are stacked: teams returning a starting quarterback for a second straight season improve by about 0.9 wins; adding the same head coach and offensive coordinator plus seven or more other offensive returnees lifts that to 1.3 wins and 53.3% ATS, with only 12 of 38 winning fewer games; and of 33 teams since 2021 with nine-plus returning offensive starters including the quarterback off a sub-45% ATS season, only three got worse. His summary is a very stable situation with a very talented roster and staff.
