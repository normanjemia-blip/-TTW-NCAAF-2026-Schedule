## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen builds this on stability and recruiting. The move to the Big 12 has produced Houston's best five-year run of recruiting classes on record, and under Willie Fritz he says a new consistent standard is being set after nine regular-season wins and a Texas Bowl win over LSU. Quarterback Conner Weigman returns as a senior after a huge year, within 19 returning starters — one of only two teams with a Stability Score of 18 on his scale, alongside Notre Dame. He adds two supporting angles: teams returning 17-plus starters have gone 55.7% ATS in the first four weeks of the last five seasons, and teams adding four or more starters year over year improved in 101 of 141 cases.
