## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen frames Toledo as the inversion of its own history. Ten teams start the season with Stability Scores of zero and Toledo is one of them, having been for a decade one of the more stable situations in the sport, known for returning starters and winning under Jason Candle. Candle's departure caused commotion that showed in recruiting: replacement Mike Jacobs's 120th-ranked class from 247Sports is almost a 40-position drop off the 16-year average. The Rockets lost far more starts and experience than the portal brought back — officially six players with starting experience, only two of them Toledo offensive players. Against a 7.5 total, and with Toledo one of only two MAC teams starting with new staffs, he notes many prognosticators still pick them first in the conference and says a decline is tough to envision avoiding.
