## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen is explicit that he is as excited as anyone to see what Brian Hartline does at South Florida, and that the reservation is about the number rather than the man. He traces Hartline's path since joining Ohio State's staff in 2017 as a receivers coach and coordinator whose players and teams were among the best in the country, and says he expects success in short order — likely as a quick hurdle to a Power Four job. But he calls first-year expectations a little over-exuberant and the 8.5 total a little aggressive, and applies the new-coach-plus-new-quarterback system: teams off a 60%-plus season with four or fewer offensive starters back and a fresh quarterback drop -3.7 wins, with the eight that also changed head coach falling 29% and -4.4 wins.
