## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen treats last season's five-win regular-season decline as unexpected and partly bad luck: four losses by 10 points or fewer, all to teams ranked in the top 25 at the time. Key players stayed, quarterback LaNorris Sellers among them. He then attaches two system angles — teams returning nine or more offensive starters including the quarterback after winning 50% or less of their games average +2.7 wins, and of 43 teams that had losing seasons despite outscoring opponents, the 17 that brought back 13-plus starters including the quarterback improved by about 20% straight up (+3.4 wins) and went 56.5% ATS. He closes on schedule: a realistic shot at heading to Florida in October at 4-1 or better, with a win there giving real momentum.
