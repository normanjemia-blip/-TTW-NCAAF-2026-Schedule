## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen credits Mark Stoops with a decade of stability at what he calls the SEC's premier basketball school — eight bowl games and 82 wins — before an embarrassing 41-0 loss to Louisville ended it. The replacement, Will Stein, is a Louisville man who played quarterback there and began coaching there 13 years ago, most recently running Oregon's offense with Dante Moore. Makinen's central point is the talent gap: there is not that type of talent at Kentucky now and there has not been on that side of the ball for a long time. Transfer additions may not make year one easier. Against a modest 4.5 total he still sees a need for an upset or two, and calls it Arkansas or Kentucky for the bottom of the SEC.
