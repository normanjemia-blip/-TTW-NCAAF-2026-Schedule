## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen's case is that the oddsmakers are giving too much credit to the strength of the new Pac-12 and that Boise State is the obvious favorite in it. He supports it with continuity — quarterback Maddux Madsen back as a senior, head coach Spencer Danielson in his fourth year — and with a schedule read that finds only one likely loss, the opener at Oregon. He states the size of his edge rather than implying it: a +1.7 win difference against the posted number, which he calls one of the biggest he has ever had, and adds that he would bet the total up to at least 8.5.
