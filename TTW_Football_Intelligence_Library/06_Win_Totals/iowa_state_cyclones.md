## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen records Matt Campbell's decade in Ames as a lasting legacy, and its cost: when Campbell left for Penn State he also took many of the players he had recruited. Jimmy Rogers, last at Washington State, starts essentially from scratch, with only two players on each side of the ball carrying any FBS starting experience — one of them a Sun Belt transfer quarterback. Most respected prognosticators put this perennial contender at the bottom of the Big 12, yet DraftKings set the total at 5.5, only 2.5 below last year's 8-4, a season after which the programme opted out of a bowl because too many players had transferred. He sees a couple of probable non-conference wins and finds it hard to see Iowa State favored in any Big 12 game, backing it with two decline systems on returning starters and on new-quarterback-plus-new-coach situations.
