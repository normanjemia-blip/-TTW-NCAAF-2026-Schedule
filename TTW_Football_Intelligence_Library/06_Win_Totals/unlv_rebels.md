## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen opens by asking the reader to go through UNLV's 2026 schedule — now without Boise State, who left for the Pac-12 — and identify any likely loss. He argues that with New Mexico's rise last year, UNLV's Mountain West consistency makes them the league favorite. Dan Mullen has picked up where Barry Odom left off, carrying 30-12 momentum over three years and using the allure of Sin City to bring in numerous Power Four transfers, including Auburn quarterback Jackson Arnold. Running the power rating against the schedule produced a 9-3 record against a total of 7.5, and he says he would have no problem betting it upward.
