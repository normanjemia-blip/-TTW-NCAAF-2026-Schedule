## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen records Charles Huff making his third coaching stop in as many years after Marshall and Southern Miss, stepping up to a Memphis programme he calls one of the better and most consistent Group of Five teams for a decade-plus. He judges the job another rung on the ladder for the oft-moving Huff, and says his name has become synonymous with instability. Memphis is picked sixth in the American by Phil Steele rather than among the elite. Only one 2025 starter is back. He applies a specific angle: of 33 teams over six seasons with four or more 20-point blowout wins that then changed head coaches, only five improved, with the group dropping 17.2% and 2.8 wins and going 44.2% ATS. The likely starting quarterback after the spring game is Marcus Stokes, a Division II West Florida transfer, and he judges this may be too big a step up for both Stokes and Huff.
