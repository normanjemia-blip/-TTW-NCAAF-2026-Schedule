## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen frames 2025 as a season played under palpable pressure, noting Texas still won 10 games despite going 5-7-1 against the spread — which he reads as evidence of how highly the bookmakers rated them. The turn came at the end: three straight wins to close, Arch Manning exploding statistically, and 456 yards in the bowl win over Michigan. He adds a first-ever No. 1 recruiting class followed by a No. 3, and one transition system: of 33 teams since 2021 with nine or more returning offensive starters including the quarterback off a season under 45% ATS, only three got worse, with the rest averaging +2.0 wins and a 16.8% ATS gain. Two more wins would put Texas at 11-1, which he calls realistic.
