## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen notes James Franklin's Penn State teams never met the fans' demands but says he has rarely been as effusive about an offseason, and that the excitement around Virginia Tech is palpable. Franklin retained 15 starters and added three more through the portal, including Penn State quarterback Ethan Grunkemeyer, who started seven games as a freshman. Two transition angles back it: teams adding four or more starters year over year improved in 101 of 141 cases, and the nowhere-to-go-but-up group of four-win teams with poor turnover ratios improved in 19 of 21. With the coaching upgrade, the returning experience and a soft early schedule, he calls the 6.5 total a little understated in an ACC full of question marks.
