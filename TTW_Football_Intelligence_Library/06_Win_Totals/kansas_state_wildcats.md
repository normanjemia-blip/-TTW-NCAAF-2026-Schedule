## VSiN's argument — TTW reference notes

Makinen's case rests on close-game arithmetic and continuity through a coaching change. Chris Klieman retired citing personal health and family after a 6-6 season, but left reasonable starting experience behind, led by third-year starting quarterback Avery Johnson. Collin Klein takes over for his first head-coaching job after seven seasons in Manhattan as an offensive assistant and two as Texas A&M's coordinator; Makinen notes he played at KSU from 2008-12, has a tremendous bond with the programme, and that the hire drew a lot of optimism. The pivot is that five of last season's six losses came by six points or fewer. With no Texas Tech or BYU on the slate he gives Kansas State an outside shot at the Big 12 title game.
